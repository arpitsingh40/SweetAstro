# Knowledge Index — Classical Canon (T1 + T2, entries 1–165)

**What this is**: the single most valuable knowledge contribution of each source in
`docs/reference_library.md`, mapped to the rule categories used by the engine.

**Confidence legend**
- `[canon]` — well-documented classical content of the text/tradition.
- `[known]` — the author's documented method or contribution.
- `[tech]` — definition of an established technique (no book-specific claim).
- `[verify]` — text/tradition real, but content summarized at domain level pending edition check.

**Rule-category keys**: promise · delay · timing_dasha · timing_transit · jaimini ·
stability · remarriage · remedies · muhurta · prasna · longevity · health · mundane.
