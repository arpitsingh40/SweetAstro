## Modern method: Karma Alignment Technique (KAT) — Rahul Kaushik

Full research note: `docs/research/rahul_kaushik_kat.md` · engine module:
`src/remedies/kat.py` · data: `data/rules/remedies_kat.json` · tests: `tests/test_kat.py`.

diagnose the life domain, then work with only that triangle's planets through
nature-aligned conduct (plus the obstructing planet) instead of trying to strengthen all
nine planets. Triangles as taught: Dharma 1-5-9 (Mars/Sun/Jupiter), Artha 2-6-10
(Venus/Mercury/Saturn), Kama 3-7-11 (Mercury/Venus/Saturn), Moksha 4-8-12
(Moon/Mars/Jupiter).

option (no efficacy claims, no guarantees, no paid-course specifics encoded). Topic
mappings that go beyond KAT's own examples are marked as SweetAstro adaptations in the
