## 3. Method — what the flagship actually claims (from its published TOC/preface)

Goel frames Hindu astrology as three pillars: **divisional charts, dasha systems, and
yogas**; the flagship covers 23 chapters across the 16 Parashari shodashavargas plus
non-Parashari and higher divisional charts (D5, D6, D8, D11, D40, D45, D60, D144…).

Techniques disclosed at TOC level (no text reviewed):

1. **Rules for examination of each divisional chart**, plus **house significations**
   and **key planets** per varga — i.e., varga-specific delineation templates.
2. **Vimshopak Bala / Varga Vimshopak Bala** and longitudes in divisional charts.
3. **"Linking of divisional charts"** — presented as the original contribution:
   intra-person linking (D1 ↔ D9 ↔ varga chain), family-member linking, and
   individual-to-individual linking (husband–wife, business partners, guru–disciple).
4. **Varga-specific applications**: D9 marriage battery (promise / early / late /
   multiple / spouse status / attraction / post-marriage / match-making), Pushkar
   Navamsha, Rashi-Tulya Navamsha and Navamsha-Tulya Rashi, 64th Navamsha; D7 children
   (sex of children, Beeja/Kshetra Sphuta); D4 fortune/settlement abroad; D10 career;
   D2 wealth variants (Parashari, Sun/Moon, cyclical); D30 affliction patterns.
5. **Dasha lens beyond Vimshottari**: dedicated works on **Chara (Jaimini), Yogini,
   Shodashottary, and Shasti Hayani** dashas for event timing, plus practical
   **Varshaphala**.
6. **Jaimini techniques**: Argala (talk 2021) and Kendrapati Dosha/Yoga (talk 2020).

## 4. Relationship to classical sources in our library

- Framework roots are classical: BPHS shodashavarga, Phaladeepika (D16 chapter
  referenced), Jaimini Sutras (chara karakas/argala), Varshaphala (Tajika).
- Goel is a **T3 modern author in the K.N. Rao lineage** (our library already lists
  K.N. Rao's Chara Dasha work, entry 206, and Jaimini material T4). His "original
  research" claims (varga linking) are **modern synthesis, not classical verses** —
  label provenance as *"V.P. Goel (modern) — <book>"* plus the classical source where
  one exists.
- The dasha systems he covers are classical (Yogini, Chara/Narayana, Shodashottary,
  Shasti Hayani); our library already has topic entries for them (315, 318, 329;
  Shodashottary is not yet listed).

## 5. Evidence assessment (honest)

- **No independent validation**: "original research" means the author's own study;
  no dataset, error rates, or controlled comparison. Review scores (Goodreads avg 4.23)
  are popularity signals, not accuracy evidence — per library policy they are not
  authority tiers.
- **No transcripts reviewed**: talk topics and methods come from publisher listings and
  speaker pages; specific rules cannot be attributed to Goel without the books.
- **Commercial products**: books sold through Sagar/Amazon/Flipkart; a pirated Scribd
  copy of the flagship exists — do not use it.
- **Same-name catalog noise** on Goodreads must be filtered when citing.
- **Ethical red line**: longevity/lifespan works (Ayu Rahasya, Calculate Your Lifespan,
  Unravelling Puzzle of Longevity) are **not adoptable** in a consumer product.
