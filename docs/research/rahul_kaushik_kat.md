## 2. The channel's own one-line definition

From the basic-course video description (`em0LMtKRnRc`, quoted):

> "The whole idea of this technique is based on the alignment of our karma in the
> right direction. We can achieve whatever we want by just putting our karma in the
> directions defined by our planet…"

In the transcripts, "direction" is used mainly in the sense of the *proper
orientation/purpose of action* (not compass directions). There is no evidence in the
captured transcripts of a compass-direction remedy system (unlike Vastu).

## 3. What KAT claims to be (as taught in the step-by-step video)

1. **BNN statements are "causative elements", not fixed fate.** The transcript states
   the base is Bhrigu Nandi Nadi, but KAT declines BNN's "pre-defined" framing:
   "in Nandi Nadi text, some things have been defined… we cannot do anything about
   pre-defined things, but in [BNN] some causative elements have been given."
   KAT re-reads those elements as levers.
2. **Align daily karma with the planet's causative nature.** Doing actions consonant
   with the planet's nature "increases the fruitfulness of that combination" — the
   remedy is action-based: "the remedy for us is not the one which we search for in
   external sources; this remedy is directly connected to you… the direction is
   connected to your actions."
3. **Skepticism of transactional remedies.** The transcript contrasts KAT with
   donation-type prescriptions ("like we see various remedies in Lal Kitab… it is not
   that they work, it is not that they do not work") and prefers conduct alignment.
4. **Modern, ethical re-expression of classical negative statements.** Example given:
   BNN says a Saturn–Moon–Rahu native earns by immoral means; KAT observes such people
   today work visibly — e.g., as food reviewers/bloggers monetising content — and
   prescribes channelling the same impulse in a modern, acceptable form.
5. **Precision by ascendant and house.** The same pair (example: Mercury–Ketu) gets
   different prescriptions depending on the houses/ascendant ("Mercury-Ketu in 1 and 5
   … in 6 and 10 — this becomes a different combination").

## 4. The core framework visible in the transcripts (Kaal Purusha triangles)

KAT groups life domains by house-triangles on the fixed zodiac (Kaal Purusha) and
works with **only the ruling planets of the relevant triangle** rather than all
nine planets:

| Domain (as taught) | Houses | Kaal Purusha signs | Planets to "balance/work" |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personality / self | 1–5–9 (Dharma) | Aries–Leo–Sagittarius | Mars, Sun, Jupiter (Moon added as mind when relevant) |
| Work / career | 2–6–10 (Artha) | Taurus–Virgo–Capricorn | Venus, Mercury, Saturn |
| Desire / business growth | 3–7–11 (Kama) | Gemini–Libra–Aquarius | Mercury, Venus, Saturn |
| Liberation / moksha | 4–8–12 | Cancer–Scorpio–Pisces | Moon, Mars, Jupiter |

Transcript example: "when it comes to balancing the work place, you have to keep three
planets in mind — Saturn, Mercury, Venus, no fourth planet is required… put the planet
which is stopping them from working, to work."

This is a coherent, teachable heuristic: **diagnose the life domain → isolate its
triangle's planets → prescribe nature-aligned actions (and address the obstructing
planet) instead of blanket strengthening of weak planets.**

## 5. Relationship to classical sources in our library

- **Bhrigu Nandi Nadi** is already in our reference library (entries 67–68; translated
  material by R.G. Rao). Classical BNN is chiefly a *transit/event-timing* Nadi system
  (Saturn/Jupiter hits on significators), not a remedy system.
- KAT's triangle mapping is a modern synthesis; its planetary nature/objects
  descriptions echo Nadi-style karakatwa catalogues, but no specific classical verse
  citations were observed in the transcripts.
- The "second house from each planet" title suggests a specific BNN-style derivative
  rule set; the captured transcripts do not detail it — it is likely covered in the
  paid course/book.

## 6. Evidence assessment (honest)

- **No independent validation**: claims of "fruitfulness increases" rest on the
  teacher's experience, testimonials and course sales pages. No dataset, no
  controlled comparison, no published error rates.
- **Auto-translated transcripts** introduce uncertainty ("Vagu Nandi Nadi" =
  Bhrigu Nandi Nadi; several terms garbled). Do not attribute precise claims to KAT
  from these transcripts without a native-language or book check.
- **Commercial interest** is explicit (₹22,900 course, membership-only videos,
  book sales). This does not invalidate the content, but it means outcome claims are
  not disinterested.
- **Direction claim is ambiguous**; the widely-quoted "directions defined by our
  planet" line refers to action-orientation in context, not spatial directions.
- KAT's own position is more calibrated than typical remedy marketing: it explicitly
  deprioritises transactional remedies and reframes deterministic BNN statements as
  actionable levers.
